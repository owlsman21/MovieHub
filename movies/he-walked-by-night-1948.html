He Walked by Night (1948)

1948 | Director: Alfred L. Werker, Anthony Mann | Genres: Crime, Film Noir, Action Classics | Quality: HD

Synopsis

When a brilliant, elusive burglar (Richard Basehart) murders a police officer in cold blood, the LAPD launches a massive manhunt utilizing early forensic science and procedural crime analysis to track him down. Co-directed by Anthony Mann with iconic high-contrast cinematography by John Alton, this groundbreaking true-crime docu-noir leads to a climactic hunt through the Los Angeles storm sewers.
