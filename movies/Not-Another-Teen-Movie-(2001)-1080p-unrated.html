Synopsis

A satirical take on classic teen films, 'Not Another Teen Movie' hilariously parodies the genre's most iconic moments and clichés. The story centers on Jake Wyler, a popular football player who makes a bet that he can turn the 'ugly duckling' Janey Briggs into the prom queen. Directed by Joel Gallen, this comedy features a cast including Chyler Leigh, Chris Evans, and Jaime Pressly, poking fun at everything from 'She's All That' to 'American Pie'.
