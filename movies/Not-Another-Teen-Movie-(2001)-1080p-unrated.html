Synopsis

A satirical take on classic teen films, 'Not Another Teen Movie' hilariously parodies the genre's most iconic moments and clichés. Directed by Joel Gallen, this comedy features a cast including Chyler Leigh, Chris Evans, and Jaime Pressly, poking fun at everything from 'She's All That' to 'American Pie'.
