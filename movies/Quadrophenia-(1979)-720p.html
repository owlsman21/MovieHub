Synopsis

Set in 1964 London, this gritty drama follows Jimmy Cooper (Phil Daniels), a young Mod disillusioned with his job and family life, who finds belonging and excitement with his scooter-riding, amphetamine-fueled gang. The film culminates in violent clashes between Mods and their rival Rockers on Brighton beach.
