Synopsis

Dive into the chilling vision of a future devoid of individuality with "THX 1138" (1971), **George Lucas's** groundbreaking directorial debut. In a subterranean world, citizens are identified by alphanumeric codes and controlled by mandatory emotion-suppressing drugs and omnipresent android police. When worker **THX 1138 (Robert Duvall)** and his roommate **LUH 3417 (Maggie McOmie)** stop taking their medication, they awaken to forbidden emotions, fall in love, and spark a desperate escape from their totalitarian society. Co-written with **Walter Murch** and produced by **Francis Ford Coppola**, this cult classic explores profound themes of control, conformity, and the fight for human freedom against technological oppression. Its stark visuals and experimental sound design influenced countless sci-fi films to follow. Experience this seminal work in stunning quality. Watch the full movie now.
