Synopsis

Based on a true story, 'Charlie Wilson's War' follows U.S. Congressman Charlie Wilson (Tom Hanks), who teams up with a wealthy Houston socialite (Julia Roberts) and a cynical CIA agent (Philip Seymour Hoffman) to launch a covert operation to arm and train the Afghan mujahideen against the Soviet Union. Directed by Mike Nichols, this political comedy-drama reveals the unlikely and often hilarious efforts that led to one of the most successful covert operations in history.
