Synopsis

Journey to a world within a world with "At the Earth's Core" (1976)! This classic Amicus Productions sci-fi fantasy adventure, directed by Kevin Connor, brings Edgar Rice Burroughs' thrilling novel to life. Join eccentric Victorian scientist Dr. Abner Perry (Peter Cushing) and his American financier David Innes (Doug McClure) as their experimental "Iron Mole" drilling machine plunges them deep into the Earth's core. They emerge into Pellucidar, a lost prehistoric land teeming with primeval beasts, primitive humans, and ruled by the malevolent, telepathic Mahars. Caroline Munro stars as the beautiful native Princess Dia, a target for sacrifice whom Innes must rescue. Renowned for its charmingly campy special effects, "rubber-suit monsters," and steampunk aesthetic, this 89-minute cult favorite offers a unique blend of adventure and fantasy.
