Synopsis

Brutes! Savages! Heroes! Dive into the brutal heart of the Congo Crisis with "Dark of the Sun" (1968), a controversial and unflinching adventure-war film. **Captain Bruce Curry (Rod Taylor)**, a hardened mercenary, is hired by the Congolese President to lead a perilous mission: travel 300 miles across war-torn territory to rescue European residents and, more importantly, retrieve a staggering $50 million in uncut diamonds from a remote mining town. Accompanied by his loyal friend **Sergeant Ruffo (Jim Brown)**, a cynical doctor (**Kenneth More**), and the ruthless ex-Nazi **Henlein (Peter Carsten)**, Curry and his team battle rival armies and internal dissent. Directed by **Jack Cardiff** and based on **Wilbur Smith's** novel, this film was notorious for its graphic violence and dark themes upon release, depicting scenes of torture and male rape. It has since gained a cult following and is cited as an influence by directors like **Quentin Tarantino** and **Martin Scorsese**. Experience this intense, action-packed mercenary classic.
