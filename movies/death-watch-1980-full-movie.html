Synopsis

Experience the chillingly prophetic vision of "Death Watch" (1980), a groundbreaking R-rated science fiction thriller directed by the acclaimed Bertrand Tavernier. In a near-future where death by disease is a rarity, Katherine Mortenhoe (Romy Schneider) becomes a morbid celebrity when diagnosed with a terminal illness. A ruthless television executive, Vincent Ferriman (Harry Dean Stanton), hires a reporter, Roddy (Harvey Keitel), who has cameras secretly implanted in his eyes to film Katherine's final days for a sensational reality TV show called "Death Watch." Based on David G. Compton's novel The Unsleeping Eye, this Franco-German co-production, also starring Max von Sydow, delves deep into themes of privacy invasion, media exploitation, and society's desensitization to suffering.
