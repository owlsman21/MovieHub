Synopsis

Hit the slopes with "Winter A-Go-Go" (1965), a groovy 60s teen comedy and musical set against the snowy backdrop of **Lake Tahoe**. When Danny Frazer (**James Stacy**), a carefree ski bum, inherits a lodge, he and his friend Jeff (**William Wellman Jr.**) decide to turn it into a happening music club. This brings together a lively cast of "ski buffs and ski babes" eager to work, play, and dance to rock 'n' roll tunes by bands like **The Nooney Rickett Four** and **The Reflections**. Directed by **Richard Benedict**, this film is a vibrant snapshot of 1960s youth culture, packed with skiing, dancing, and lighthearted romance.
